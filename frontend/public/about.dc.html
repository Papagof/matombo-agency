Our story

Local insight beats borrowed playbooks.

We started Matombo after watching brilliant Lagos venues lose bookings to slick listings and louder budgets. The problem was rarely the product — it was marketing that didn't understand the local buyer.

So we built an agency that does. We know how a guest searches for a hotel in Lagos, how a couple chooses a lounge for the weekend, and how a family decides on a home. We turn that understanding into demand you can measure.
